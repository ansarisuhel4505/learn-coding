Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: February 20, 2026

At CodeMaster, we want you to be fully satisfied with your learning experience. If you are not completely happy with a course, we offer a transparent and fair refund policy.

1. The 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We provide a 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee for all our standard courses. If you request a refund within 7 days of your purchase, we will refund 100% of your payment.

2. Conditions for Refund Eligibility

To prevent misuse of our platform, refunds are only approved if the following conditions are met:

  • The refund request is initiated within 7 days of the transaction.
  • You have consumed less than 10% of the total course content.
  • You have not downloaded any premium source code or study materials.
  • You have not completed any online exams or claimed a certificate for the course.

3. Non-Refundable Items

Please note that the following purchases are strictly non-refundable:

  • 1-on-1 Mentorship Sessions or Live Classes.
  • Course bundles or lifetime access passes bought during special flash sales.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please send an email to refunds@codemaster.com from your registered email ID with your payment receipt and the reason for the refund. Our team will verify your course progress and process the refund to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.
