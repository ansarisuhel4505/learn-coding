Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: February 20, 2026

At CodeMaster, we want you to be fully satisfied with your learning experience. If you are not completely happy with a course, we offer a transparent, hassle-free, and fair refund policy.

1. The 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We provide a strictly monitored 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee for all our standard online courses. If you request a refund within 7 days of your successful purchase, we will refund 100% of your payment—no questions asked.

2. Conditions for Refund Eligibility

To prevent piracy and misuse of our platform, refunds are only approved if the following conditions are strictly met:

  • The refund request is initiated within exactly 7 days of the transaction.
  • You have consumed less than 10% of the total course video content.
  • You have not downloaded any premium source code or study materials (PDFs/Notes).
  • You have not completed any online proctored exams or claimed a certificate for the course.

3. Non-Refundable Items

Please note that the following purchases are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances:

  • 1-on-1 Mentorship Sessions or Live Cohort Classes.
  • Course bundles or lifetime access passes bought during special flash sales.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please send an email to refunds@codemaster.com from your registered email ID. Include your payment receipt and a brief reason for the refund.

Our billing team will verify your course progress and process the refund to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.
