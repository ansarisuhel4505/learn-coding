Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 28, 2026

At CodeMaster, accessible from our official platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our students and visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains the types of information that is collected and recorded by CodeMaster and how we securely use it.

1. Information We Collect

The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to provide your personal information.

  • Account Registration Details: Your Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Number, and Roll Number.
  • Security Data: Encrypted (Hashed) Passwords to ensure your account's ultimate safety.
  • Usage Data: Course progress, submitted exam answers, and AI Chatbot interactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our educational website.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our platform to suggest better courses.
  • Analyze your exam performance to generate accurate Live Ranks and Leaderboards.
  • Send you emails regarding security alerts, OTP verifications, and password resets.

Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our data protection team at support@codemaster.com.
